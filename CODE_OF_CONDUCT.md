# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a project where people can participate without harassment,
regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful behavior directly.
- Accept feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Keep private information and credentials out of public discussions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, and sharing
another person's private information are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed
to keep project spaces safe.

Report conduct concerns privately to [team@steel.dev](mailto:team@steel.dev).
Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
